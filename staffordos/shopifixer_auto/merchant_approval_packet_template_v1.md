# Merchant Approval Packet Template v1

Status: TEMPLATE_ONLY_NOT_IMPLEMENTED

Purpose: merchant-facing structure for presenting a sandboxed ShopiFixer theme improvement. Keep language professional, trustworthy, and non-technical.

## Title

Preview change for review

## What Changed

Briefly describe the visible change in plain language.

Example:

We adjusted the product page preview so the purchase area is easier to understand on desktop and mobile.

## Why It Changed

Explain the business reason without hype.

Example:

The audit showed that shoppers may not be getting enough clarity before deciding whether to add the product to cart. This change is intended to make the next step clearer.

## Expected Outcome

State the expected direction, not a guarantee.

Example:

The expected outcome is a clearer product page that makes it easier for shoppers to continue toward purchase.

## Proof Reviewed

List the available proof:

- before screenshot
- after screenshot
- visual comparison
- changed-file summary
- rollback plan

## Rollback Safety

Use plain language:

This was prepared in a preview copy. Your live store does not need to change unless you approve it. If the preview is rejected, the change can be discarded or rolled back from the saved original.

## Approval Request

Please review the preview and choose one:

- Approved to launch
- Approved with modifications
- Needs review
- Rejected

## Notes

Record merchant concerns, requested modifications, or approval bounds.
